# Adana Kebab

## Ingredients
- 1 onion
- 1 bell pepper
- Healthy amount of parsley
- 1 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp kebab seasoning
- 1 lb ground meat (originally used 90/10 beef)

## Recipe
1. Mince the onion, bell pepper and parsley together
2. Drain the liquid
3. Combine with kosher salt, kebab seasoning, and ground meat
4. Portion in the palm of your hand, emphasis on flat and try for thinness
5. Air fryer 12 minutes @ 375(F)
