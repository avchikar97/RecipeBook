# Lemon Crem Posset

## Ingredients
- 3 lemons (optional)
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2.5 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/3 cup sugar
- Splash of vanilla extract
- Lemon zest

## Recipe
1. If you're using lemons as cups, spoon them out now. This can take a while. Otherwise, skip this step.
2. Juice the lemons
3. Add the heavy cream, sugar, and vanilla extract to a saucepan and boil. Stir until the sugar dissolves completely and let soft boil for about 5 minutes.
4. Let cool for about 1 minute
5. Add lemon juice
6. Add to cups
7. Let cool and serve
